Privacy Policy

For Baby Prediction / GoodNews Baby
Effective date: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Prediction (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the Baby Prediction website and app to create baby-celebration games, invite participants, submit predictions and view results.

Please note: Do not enter sensitive personal information about yourself or a child into game fields. Game creators should only share details they have permission to share.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account information: information used to create and access an account, such as your email address and authentication/session information.
  • Game information: game titles, questions, settings, names or other details that a creator chooses to enter.
  • Predictions and participation: participant names or nicknames, submitted answers/predictions, and related game activity.
  • Support communications: information you include when you contact us for help.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, security and server-log information that may be processed by our hosting or service providers to operate and protect the service.

Please avoid entering medical, financial, government-identification or other sensitive information. Baby Prediction is a celebration and prediction game, not a medical service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and operate accounts and games, save and display predictions and results, enable sharing with invited participants, provide support, maintain security, prevent misuse, and fix or improve the service.

3. How information is stored and shared

Information is processed by service providers that help operate the app, including Supabase for application data and authentication and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information. Information may be visible to a game creator or participants where that visibility is part of the game's features or sharing settings. Anyone with access to a shared game link may be able to view the information made available through that link, so share links thoughtfully.

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the service, or address security and fraud issues.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain game history, meet legitimate operational needs and comply with legal obligations. You can request account or personal-data deletion by contacting us at Linkcardsapp@gmail.com or using the account-deletion feature in the app, where available. We may retain limited information where required by law or needed to resolve disputes, enforce our terms or protect security. Deleting an account may remove access to associated games and information.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children’s privacy

Baby Prediction is intended for adults, such as parents, family members and friends organising or participating in baby-celebration games. It is not designed for children to create accounts. Adults should not ask children to provide personal information through the service and should take care when entering or sharing information about a child.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or request a copy of personal information, or object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at the email below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes or legal requirements evolve. We will publish the latest version on this page and update the effective date.
